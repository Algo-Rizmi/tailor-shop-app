Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Tailor Shop app ("the app") collects, how it is used, and your choices regarding that information.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address, used to create and secure your account. If you choose "Continue with Google," we receive your email address and a Google account identifier — we never receive or store your Google password.
  • Shop data: the receipts, clothing items, and volume/numbering information you enter while using the app. This data is private to your account and not visible to any other shop using the app.

How We Use Information

  • To create and authenticate your account.
  • To store and display the receipts and shop data you enter.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Data Storage & Security

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database with encrypted connections. Passwords are never stored in plain text — they are hashed before storage. Account sessions use industry-standard token-based authentication.

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your account and shop data for as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email address below.

Third-Party Services

The app uses Google Sign-In as an optional login method. If used, Google's own privacy policy also applies to the information Google processes as part of that sign-in flow.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
