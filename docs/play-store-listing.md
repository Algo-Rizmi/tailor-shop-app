## Full description (max 4000 characters)
Tailor Shop Receipts replaces your paper receipt book with a simple, fast app built
specifically for tailor and alteration shops.

**How it works**
Pick a starting number for your receipt book (just like the first page of a paper one), and
every new receipt after that numbers itself automatically — no more manual counting, no more
duplicate numbers, even with multiple phones or tablets in the same shop.

**Built for real shop work**
• Record customer name and phone (optional — perfect for walk-ins)
• Add clothing items with type, color, and quantity — shirts, pants, jackets, dresses, and more,
  or describe anything custom
• Track payment status and due dates
• Search and filter receipts by status
• Start a new "chapter" (volume) any time you fill a book, with full history kept

**Your own private shop**
Every shop that signs up gets its own completely private space — your receipts are never
visible to any other shop using the app. Sign up with email and password, or continue with
your Google account.

**Reliable by design**
Receipt numbers are assigned by a database-guaranteed atomic operation, so two devices tapping
"New Receipt" at the exact same instant can never end up with the same number — tested under
real concurrent load.
